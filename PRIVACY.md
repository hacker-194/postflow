# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: 2024-01-01
**Last Updated**: 2024-01-01

Free Group Poster ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the Extension collects, how it's used, and your rights.

## Data We Collect

### Local Data Only

All data is stored **locally in your browser** using IndexedDB. No data is transmitted to any external server, including our own.

The Extension stores:

- **Group Lists**: Names, IDs, and URLs of Facebook/WhatsApp/Messenger groups you choose to save
- **Templates**: Caption templates you create for reuse
- **Image Sets**: Image URLs you save for sharing
- **Scheduled Shares**: Posts you schedule for future sharing
- **Share History**: Records of completed/failed shares (author, groups, timestamps, status)
- **Settings**: Your preferences (theme, safety level, notification options, etc.)
- **Daily Counters**: Post counts for rate limiting

### No Personal Identifiers

The Extension does **not** collect:

- Your name, email, or account credentials
- Facebook session cookies or access tokens
- Browsing history outside of explicit group sync actions
- Analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics (unless you explicitly opt-in)

## How Data Is Used

- **Group Lists**: To let you select groups when sharing
- **Templates/Images**: To pre-fill the share sheet
- **Scheduled Shares**: To execute shares at your chosen time
- **History**: To show you past sharing activity
- **Settings**: To configure the Extension's behavior
- **Daily Counters**: To enforce safety limits

## Data Sharing

**We do not share your data with anyone.** No third parties, no analytics providers, no advertisers. The Extension makes network requests only to:

- Facebook domains (`*.facebook.com`, `*.fbcdn.net`, `*.scontent*.xx.fbcdn.net`) — for sharing posts
- WhatsApp Web (`web.whatsapp.com`) — for group sync
- Messenger (`messenger.com`) — for group sync

These requests are initiated by **you** when you click "Sync Groups" or "Share Now".

## Data Retention

- Data persists until you delete it via the Extension's "Clear All Data" option
- Uninstalling the Extension removes all local data
- No cloud backup or sync — data stays on your device

## Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

- **View**: Open the Extension popup/options to see all stored data
- **Export**: Use "Export Data" to download a JSON backup
- **Import**: Use "Import Data" to restore from backup
- **Delete**: Use "Clear All Data" to wipe everything
- **Opt-out**: Analytics opt-in is disabled by default

## Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted in the Extension's release notes. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on GitHub or email the maintainers.

**TL;DR**: Your data stays on your device. We don't see it, sell it, or share it. You own it completely.

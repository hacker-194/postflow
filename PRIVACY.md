# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2024**

Free Group Poster ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the Extension collects, how it's used, and your rights.

## Data We Collect

**We do not collect any personal data.** The Extension operates entirely on your device.

### Local Data Stored (IndexedDB)
The following data is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB and never leaves your device:

- **Group Lists**: Names, colors, and Facebook group IDs you create
- **Post Templates**: Caption templates and sharing settings you save
- **Image Sets**: Image URLs you provide for rotation
- **Sharing History**: Records of past sharing activities (status, timestamps, group counts)
- **Scheduled Shares**: Pending and completed scheduled sharing jobs
- **User Settings**: Theme preference, notification settings, share button position
- **Daily Post Counter**: Count of posts shared today (resets daily)

### Data We Do NOT Collect
- No personal identifiers (name, email, Facebook account info)
- No browsing history or Facebook activity
- No analytics or telemetry (unless you explicitly opt-in)
- No cookies or tracking pixels
- No data sent to external servers

## How Data Is Used

All data is used **exclusively** to provide the Extension's functionality:
- Display your saved groups, templates, and images in the options page
- Execute sharing to selected Facebook groups
- Enforce safety limits (daily caps, overnight pauses, delays)
- Persist your preferences across browser sessions

## Data Sharing

**We do not share your data with anyone.** The Extension:
- Makes no external network requests except to `facebook.com`/`fbcdn.net` domains (required for sharing)
- Does not use third-party analytics, crash reporting, or monitoring services
- Has no backend server or database

## Your Rights & Control

- **Export**: Use "Export Data" in Options → Settings to download a JSON backup
- **Import**: Use "Import Data" to restore from a backup file
- **Delete All**: Use "Clear All Data" in Options → Settings to wipe everything
- **Uninstall**: Removing the Extension deletes all IndexedDB data automatically

## Optional Analytics (Opt-In Only)

If you enable "Anonymous usage analytics" in Settings:
- Only aggregate, non-identifiable usage statistics may be collected
- No personal data, group names, or post content is included
- You can disable this at any time

## Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted in the Extension's release notes. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/free-group-poster/free-group-poster
